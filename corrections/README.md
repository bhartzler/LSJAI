# The corrections, with their evidence

Tabs, UTF-8, a header row, one row a line.

- `corrections.tsv`: `entry` (Perseus's `orig_id`), `headword`, `item` (the reading's id: unit and span), `applied` (which round), `line` (the line in the one-file copy the applier read), `old`, `new`, `verdict` (`B`: copy B has the print, Logeion does not; `both-wrong`: neither had it, the print's text given), `how sure` (the reader's certainty, 1 to 5), `why`, `evidence source` (copy B; Logeion; or the readers' record of a page of the print or the Supplement, by its file name), `evidence quote` (the print's words as the readers' record gives them; copy B's reading is not quoted, it is the `new` text), `in LSJAI` (`replaced Logeion's`: Logeion's line was unchanged since the copy read and our corrected line replaced it; `carried onto Logeion's`: our corrected span carried onto a line Logeion had changed elsewhere; `already Logeion's`: Logeion had made the same correction; `Logeion's kept`: a later Logeion edit and ours touch the same place, so Logeion's line stands). "Ours" throughout means the corrections of Bruce Hartzler's AI agents; "Logeion's" the text of LSJLogeion.. 1,663 rows: 1,634 from the readings, on 1,510 entries, and 29 spans on 28 entries where typo fixes from copy B's own history were carried over (`applied` 23 Oct 2026, `item` copyB-2020).
- `held.tsv`: 101 corrections the readers established that the applier held: `why held` says what stopped it (the span crosses a tag or a sense boundary, the line had changed since the reading, two spans overlap); `old` and `new` are the text as read and the text the print has; the night's verdict and reason beside them.
- `residue-c.tsv`: 34 citations that AI readers confirmed on 31 Oct 2026 among the spans `tools/mark_citations.py` could not settle, held unmarked: `printed` is the citation as the reader read it, `reference` the reader's reference (a Perseus n, or the citation in LSJ's abbreviations), `held because` what stops a `<bibl>` there (it would cross an element that also holds the punctuation after the citation, 15 of them an `<i>` with a face letter or a Latin word after `s.v.`, which the dictionary never closes before the stop; hold a tag the dictionary's bibls never hold, a `<cit>` opening inside it or a `<placeName>`; or stand where the dictionary has no bibl), `the rule's reason` why the rule listed it; `id` and `span` are the item's in the readers' package.
- `markup.tsv`: the 208 places where the dictionary's markup was moved, its text unchanged byte for byte, so that a citation the readers confirmed could be marked: the `</cit>` of the quotation before a citation, which closed inside it, moved to after the citation (200), and the stop or comma after a citation, which its `<foreign>` held, moved out of it (8); `from` and `to` are the markup before and after, `why` the fault.
- `heads.tsv`: the pass that makes a head's visible text follow its `orth_orig`: 54 followed, 68 kept with the reason.
- `merge-report.tsv`: every entry carried onto LSJLogeion's HEAD, with its file and how (the same four words).

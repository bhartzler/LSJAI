# The corrections, with their evidence

Tabs, UTF-8, a header row, one row a line.

- `corrections.tsv`: `entry` (Perseus's `orig_id`), `headword`, `item` (the reading's id: unit and span), `applied` (which round), `line` (the line in the one-file copy the applier read), `old`, `new`, `verdict` (`B`: copy B has the print, Logeion does not; `both-wrong`: neither had it, the print's text given), `how sure` (the reader's certainty, 1 to 5), `why`, `evidence source` (copy B; Logeion; or the readers' record of a page of the print or the Supplement, by its file name), `evidence quote` (the print's words as the readers' record gives them; copy B's reading is not quoted, it is the `new` text), `in LSJAI` (`replaced Logeion's`: Logeion's line was unchanged since the copy read and our corrected line replaced it; `carried onto Logeion's`: our corrected span carried onto a line Logeion had changed elsewhere; `already Logeion's`: Logeion had made the same correction; `Logeion's kept`: a later Logeion edit and ours touch the same place, so Logeion's line stands). "Ours" throughout means the corrections of Bruce Hartzler's AI agents; "Logeion's" the text of LSJLogeion.. 1,257 rows: 1,228 from the readings, on 1,140 entries, and 29 spans on 28 entries where typo fixes from copy B's own history were carried over (`applied` 23 Oct 2026, `item` copyB-2020).
- `held.tsv`: 81 corrections the readers established that the applier held: `why held` says what stopped it (the span crosses a tag or a sense boundary, the line had changed since the reading, two spans overlap); `old` and `new` are the text as read and the text the print has; the night's verdict and reason beside them.
- `heads.tsv`: the pass that makes a head's visible text follow its `orth_orig`: 37 followed, 48 kept with the reason.
- `merge-report.tsv`: every entry carried onto LSJLogeion's HEAD, with its file and how (the same four words).

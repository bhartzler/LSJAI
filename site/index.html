About

How it works

  1. AI readers check each entry against the evidence: the printed page, the 1968 Supplement, other copies of the text, and the passages it cites.
  2. A second AI reader reviews and resolves disagreements.
  3. Every correction can be proofread by anyone: the old text, the new text, the reader's reason, and the evidence.
  4. Under each citation is the text cited, with every edition as a witness, an apparatus of their differences, and the translations.
  5. The next pass with better AI readers will find what this one missed. Every error has a shelf life.
